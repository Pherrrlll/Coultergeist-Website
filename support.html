Common questions

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by the store you bought the game from. Request a refund through Steam, Google Play or the App Store, following that store's refund policy.

Will my progress transfer between devices?

Your progress is saved on your own device. Steam Cloud, Android backup or iCloud backup may keep a copy if you've turned them on, but progress can't be moved between different platforms.

How do I reset my progress?

Delete the game's save data: uninstall and reinstall the game, or clear the game's app data on Android.

What data do your games collect?

None. Read the details in our privacy policies.
